Company Profile

Flowbiz Exports Pvt. Ltd. is a private limited company dealing in industrial valves and flow-control equipment. We serve domestic and international customers with dependable products, clear communication and on-time delivery.

Our team understands the needs of project buyers, distributors and plant maintenance teams, and helps them select the right valve for their application.
